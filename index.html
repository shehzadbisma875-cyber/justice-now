Privacy Policy

Justice Now respects your privacy and is designed to help you control your personal information.

1. Information

The app may use information that you provide, such as your name, contact information, case information and files you choose to store.

2. Location

Location access is requested only when a feature requires it, such as location sharing or emergency assistance.

3. Evidence

Evidence uploaded to the app should be treated as sensitive information. A production version should use secure authentication, encryption and protected server storage.

4. User Control

Users should be able to manage permissions, privacy settings and stored information through the application.
